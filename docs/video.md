# Phase Two — 3-Minute Video Script

~442 words of narration, timed to roughly 3 minutes at a natural pace (~140–150 wpm).

## [0:00–0:15] — Hook

**Visual:** Two people shaking hands over a laptop, then a question mark over the handshake.

**VO:**
Every time you pay someone before the work is done, you're trusting them. And every time someone works before they're paid, they're trusting you. That trust usually needs a third party. A platform. A middleman. A fee. Phase Two removes that middleman — and replaces it with AI.

## [0:15–0:40] — What it is

**Visual:** Phase Two landing page, then a simple diagram: Depositor → Escrow → Counterparty.

**VO:**
Phase Two is an escrow app built on GenLayer. Here's the idea. One person, the depositor, locks a payment in a smart contract. They write exactly what they expect — in plain English. The other person, the counterparty, does the work and submits proof. Then independent AI validators check that proof against the spec — and release the payment automatically, if it holds up. No arbitrator. No support ticket. No "trust me."

## [0:40–1:15] — How it works

**Visual:** Screen recording — Create Engagement, then Accept, then Submit Deliverable.

**VO:**
Let's walk through it. You create an engagement: who you're paying, what you expect, and a deadline. The other side has to actually accept it first — no one gets pulled into work they didn't agree to. And if they decline, your deposit comes right back. Once they accept, they do the work and submit evidence — a link, a repo, a live page, whatever proves it's done. Then anyone can trigger judgment. Five independent validators fetch that evidence themselves, right then, and compare it to the spec. They don't just trust one model's opinion — they have to agree with each other first.

## [1:15–1:45] — Trust, without exposure

**Visual:** Comment thread with a lock icon animating in; a "suggest changes" button highlighted.

**VO:**
Now, work isn't just code and deadlines — it's conversation. So Phase Two has a private messaging thread on every engagement. And we mean private: every message is end-to-end encrypted, using keys generated from your own wallet. Not even we can read them. If terms need to change before you commit, you can suggest an edit instead of just walking away.

## [1:45–2:15] — Flexibility and trust signals

**Visual:** Milestone plan with three linked payments; a reputation card next to a wallet address.

**VO:**
Big projects don't have to be all-or-nothing. You can split a payment into milestones — each one its own contract, its own proof, its own judgment. Get paid as you go. And before you commit to working with someone, you can check their track record — approval rate, dispute history — pulled straight from the chain, not a review someone could fake.

## [2:15–2:45] — Transparency

**Visual:** Transparency dashboard — hero number, bar charts filling in, table scrolling.

**VO:**
Everything Phase Two does is visible. The Transparency page shows every engagement, every GEN moved, and exactly how each case was decided — live, straight from the contract. Nothing hidden, nothing cached. Because a system that asks you to trust it should be easy to check.

## [2:45–3:00] — Close

**Visual:** Logo, tagline, URL.

**VO:**
Phase Two: payment that releases itself, once the work is proven. No middleman. No waiting on someone's word. Just the work, the evidence, and an honest verdict. Try it today.
