## [0:10–0:25] What GenLayer is

**Visual:** GenLayer homepage, then a quick transition into the Phase Two landing page.

**VO:**
GenLayer is a blockchain that lets smart contracts use AI to make real judgment calls, instead of just following rigid code. In Phase Two, that's exactly what powers the whole thing: independent AI validators that actually check whether work was done, instead of trusting someone's word for it.

## [0:25–1:15] What Phase Two is

**Visual:** Screen recording, in order — Create Engagement form, Accept, Submit Deliverable, then the judgment/release moment.

**VO:**
Phase Two is a peer to peer escrow platform built on GenLayer. Here's how it works. You create an engagement: the counterparty's wallet address, the amount you're depositing, what you want done, and the criteria it'll actually be judged against. The counterparty accepts, does the work, and submits evidence, a link, a repo, whatever proves it. Independent AI validators then fetch that evidence themselves and compare it to your criteria. If it holds up, payment releases automatically. If not, or if either side disagrees, there's a dispute process, so nobody's stuck with a bad call and no way out.
